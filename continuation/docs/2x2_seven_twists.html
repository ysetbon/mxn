Reading the drawings

Whites and greens are the two horizontal sets, indigos the two vertical sets; every set keeps its colour outward, so any strand can be followed from the core to the seventh ring. All plates share one viewport — the stitch genuinely grows. Labels mark the newest ring's arms, from _4/_5 out to _16/_17.

Two things make depth work: the virtual relabel composes through each level's map, so every ring presents to the engine as an honest starting stitch and the k machinery — grouping, pairing, masks — applies natively at any depth; and each level's extensions are seeded from level 1's answer for its own k with a small bounded search around it, which keeps arms at level-1 scale — every level of this chain sits in the 20–100px range.
